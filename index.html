Better Golf Starts With Better Practice

Most golfers do not need more time at the range. They need a better plan for the time they already have. Prairie Pin Practice Lab is a small indoor and outdoor practice studio in the town of Cedar Hollow, built around one simple idea: every bucket of balls should have a goal.

Whether you are holding a club for the first time or trying to break 80, our coaches help you choose the right tools, use drills that transfer to the course, and track progress you can actually see.

Our practice philosophy

We build every lesson and every practice plan around three habits.

1. Pick one target

Hitting balls with no target trains you to swing, not to score. Every shot in our studio goes at something specific, even if it is just a flag on the far fence.

2. Keep score

A drill without a score is just exercise. We give every drill a simple pass mark so you can see improvement from week to week.

3. Practice like you play

On the course you get one try with each club. We finish every session by changing clubs and targets on every shot, the same way a real round feels.

Coach's tip: Spend at least half of your practice time within 50 yards of the hole. For most golfers, that is where the easiest strokes are saved.
